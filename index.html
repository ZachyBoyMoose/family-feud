Cast to your TV (optional)

  1. Open the TV Board. not connected It opens in its own window (?view=tv). Click once inside it to turn on sound and go fullscreen. Don't minimize it; it can sit behind this window.
  2. Cast that tab. Chrome: in the TV Board window, ⋮ menu → Cast, save, and share → Cast… → Sources → Cast tab → pick your TV.
    Edge: ⋯ menu → More tools → Cast media to device.
    Then aim and shoot from this window. The TV Board mirrors every shot live.
