📺 Quick Living-Room Cast Setup

  1. Launch the TV Board. It opens in a new tab. Click that tab once so it can play sound effects.
    📺 Launch TV Board
  2. Cast that tab to your TV. In Chrome or Edge, open the browser menu ⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Cast…, then under Sources choose Cast tab (not Cast screen) and pick your TV while the TV Board tab is active.
    Older Edge versions: ⋯ → More tools → Cast media to device. The board auto-scales to any 16:9 screen, from 720p up to 4K.
  3. Keep this Host Controller tab on your laptop. Players only see the TV board. The answers and controls stay here with you.

✨ AI Surveys & Answer Judge Optional

Paste a Google Gemini API key for endless fresh surveys and an AI "close enough" judge. The key is saved only in this browser (localStorage) and is only ever sent to Google's Gemini API.

How to get a free key from Google AI Studio
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key, then copy it.
  3. Paste it above and click Save & Test Key.
The free tier has rate limits. If you hit one mid-game, the app tells you and switches to the built-in surveys.
